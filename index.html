What to know before you join.

The short answers people ask first. More will follow as PoolIt opens.

Can I share Netflix or Spotify with people outside my home?

Only as each service allows. Netflix lets you add paid extra members outside your household, at ₦2,500 a month each and up to two on Premium. Spotify Family is meant for up to six people at the same address. Check a plan's rules before you pool it.

How is my share worked out?

It is the plan price split between the people in the group. Spotify Family at ₦2,500 across six people is about ₦417 each. Netflix Premium adds two paid extra-member slots, so a group of five splits ₦13,500 between them, which is ₦2,700 each.

When am I charged?

Your share is collected automatically every month. You link a card once, so nobody has to chase transfers.

Which services can I pool?

Netflix, Spotify, YouTube Premium, Google One, Canva, Prime Video, Apple Music and more. Any plan that comes with family or group seats can work, each under its own rules.
